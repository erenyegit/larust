# Larust Pitch Variants

## 50-Word Pitch
Larust is a Walrus-native feedback vault for product teams. Public users submit bug reports, feature requests, and surveys without wallet friction. Admins use Sui wallet ownership to triage submissions, prioritize work, and export evidence. Sensitive fields follow Seal encryption paths, making feedback both actionable and verifiable.

## 100-Word Pitch
Larust turns feedback collection into a trustworthy evidence workflow. Teams create schema-driven forms for bug reports, feature requests, and surveys. Respondents submit without wallet friction, including screenshots and videos. Every submission is stored on Walrus as canonical evidence, while local indexing powers fast triage operations. Sensitive fields are explicitly marked and routed through Seal encryption paths when configured. Admins authenticate with Sui wallet signatures for owner-gated workflows such as notes, prioritization, filtering, and exports. The result is a productized feedback command center: frictionless on the front door, cryptographically credible in the backend, and operationally sharp for teams making roadmap decisions.

## 200-Word Pitch
Larust is built for teams that need more than a generic form builder. It is a Walrus-native feedback vault that captures high-signal product evidence and keeps it verifiable end-to-end.

Public respondents can submit bug reports, feature requests, and surveys with no wallet requirement, which preserves response volume and quality. The form studio is schema-driven and supports rich text, dropdowns, checkbox groups, star ratings, URLs, screenshots, and video uploads. Form owners can configure required and sensitive fields, helper copy, and template-based starting points.

Under the hood, Larust stores submission payloads and media references on Walrus as canonical blobs, while a local relational index enables fast dashboard operations. This split gives teams both durability and speed: trustworthy evidence plus practical triage UX.

For sensitive information, Larust separates public and sensitive payloads and integrates Seal encryption paths (client-first when configured, with server fallback in constrained environments). Admin workflows are owner-gated through Sui wallet signature sessions, so notes, prioritization, exports, and review actions are protected by explicit ownership.

In short, Larust combines frictionless intake, cryptographic evidence integrity, and operational triage discipline in one polished product — exactly what modern product teams need to trust feedback at scale.
