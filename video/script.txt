Larust — Walrus Sessions 2026 — 2-minute narration

[0:00 · Hook]
Most feedback tools collect text. Larust collects evidence.

[0:06 · What it is]
We built Larust for the Walrus Sessions hackathon. It's a feedback and form platform where every submission becomes a canonical record on Walrus, with sensitive fields protected by Seal.

[0:24 · Problem]
Today, product teams scatter feedback across email, chat, and forgotten screenshots. Important details get lost, ownership is fuzzy, and there's no durable record of what was actually reported.

[0:42 · Public intake]
Larust fixes the intake side first. Anyone can open a public link and submit without ever connecting a wallet. Forms support rich text, dropdowns, checkboxes, star ratings, screenshots, video uploads, URLs, and required fields — every input type a modern feedback tool needs.

[1:04 · Walrus storage]
Behind the scenes, every submission payload and every uploaded asset is written to Walrus. The returned blob ID becomes the canonical reference — durable, retrievable, and verifiable.

[1:23 · Seal sensitive]
Fields the owner marks as sensitive are isolated at submit time and encrypted with Seal before the canonical write. Public answers stay queryable; private answers stay protected.

[1:41 · Admin dashboard]
Form owners connect their Sui wallet, sign a session, and unlock a private dashboard. From there they triage submissions, filter by status, priority, or date, add notes, set urgency, and export as JSON or CSV.

[2:00 · Close]
Walletless intake. Walrus-native evidence. Seal-protected secrets. Wallet-gated admin. That's Larust.
